# India Leave Addendum — GSVH India Pvt Ltd

This addendum defines the leave entitlements for employees of GSVH India Pvt Ltd. Where this addendum differs from the global GSVH Corp leave policies (`time-and-leave/leave-policies.md` and `time-and-leave/working-hours-and-pto.md`), this addendum governs for India employees. Where this addendum is silent, the global policy applies.

India leave entitlements are shaped by central and state labour law, including the Shops and Establishments Act applicable to GSVH India Pvt Ltd's registered state (Karnataka) and other states where employees are based. Where applicable law provides entitlements greater than this addendum, applicable law governs.

## 1. Casual Leave (CL)

India employees are entitled to **12 casual leave days per calendar year**. Casual leave is for unforeseen personal needs — a sudden illness, a personal emergency, a short personal errand. It cannot be accumulated or carried over to the next calendar year; unused casual leave lapses on 31 December.

Casual leave is taken in minimum half-day units. Employees notify their manager on the same day (or in advance where possible). Supporting documentation is not required for casual leave of up to 2 consecutive days. Casual leave cannot be combined with other leave types (earned leave or sick leave) without HR approval.

New joiners who join during the calendar year receive casual leave on a pro-rated basis: 1 day per complete calendar month remaining in the year from the date of joining.

## 2. Sick Leave (SL)

India employees are entitled to **12 sick leave days per calendar year**. Sick leave is for genuine illness or injury. Employees must notify their manager on the morning of the first absent day. For absences of 3 or more consecutive sick leave days, a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required and must be submitted within 5 days of return to work.

Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on separation. The company's global sick leave policy (10 days under `time-and-leave/leave-policies.md`) is supplemented to 12 days for India employees under this addendum.

## 3. Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave (PL)

India employees are entitled to **18 earned leave days per calendar year**, accruing at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Earned leave is the India equivalent of the global PTO entitlement; it is pro-rated in the joining year. Employees begin accruing earned leave from their start date.

Carryover: earned leave may be carried forward subject to a maximum carry-forward balance of **30 days**. Leave in excess of 30 days in the balance at year-end is lapsed (where permitted by applicable state law) unless the employee is unable to take leave due to business requirements, in which case the lapse may be reviewed by HR.

Leave encashment: India employees may encash a maximum of **10 earned leave days per calendar year** during service (i.e., without leaving the company), subject to maintaining a minimum balance of 10 days. Encashment is processed in the April payroll cycle. On separation, all accrued and unused earned leave is encashed in the Full and Final settlement at the last drawn basic salary.

Earned leave applications of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through Workday. Applications of fewer than 3 days should be submitted at least 2 working days ahead where possible; last-minute earned leave requires manager approval.

## 4. Public Holidays

GSVH India Pvt Ltd observes **10 public holidays per calendar year**, published in the India Holiday Calendar each December for the following year. The calendar includes:
- National holidays: Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October).
- Regional and other holidays: a selection of 7 additional holidays, which may vary by office location (Bengaluru office may observe Karnataka-specific holidays; other locations observe relevant regional holidays).

Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off (Compensatory Off, CompOff) in lieu, to be taken within 30 days of the working public holiday with manager approval.

## 5. Maternity Leave (India)

Maternity leave for India employees is governed by the **Maternity Benefit Act 1961** as amended. Eligible female employees are entitled to **26 weeks of paid maternity leave** for the first two children and 12 weeks for the third child onwards. Maternity leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date. To be eligible, the employee must have worked at GSVH India Pvt Ltd for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.

GSVH India Pvt Ltd pays full salary during maternity leave as required by the Act. Employees may also be entitled to a nursing break and crèche facility rights under the Act; HR will advise on local implementation.

Miscarriage leave of 6 weeks with pay is also provided under the Act for a miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy.

## 6. Paternity and Parental Leave (India)

India does not currently have a central statutory paternity leave law. GSVH India Pvt Ltd provides **10 working days of paid paternity leave** to non-birthing parents (including adoptive parents) to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption, consistent with the company's global parental leave commitment. This leave is in addition to casual leave entitlements.

Employees adopting a child of less than 3 months of age are entitled to **12 weeks of adoption leave** for the primary carer (equivalent to the maternity leave provision for third and subsequent children).

## 7. Bereavement Leave (India)

India employees receive the same bereavement leave entitlement as the global policy: up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member and up to 2 paid days for an extended family member. Given the significance of bereavement rites in Indian culture, managers are encouraged to extend compassion and flexibility beyond the minimum entitlement where business conditions allow; additional time off can be arranged as casual or earned leave.

## 8. Compensatory Off (CompOff)

When an India employee works on a declared public holiday or on a weekly off (Saturday/Sunday) due to an explicit business requirement authorized by the manager, they earn one compensatory off day. CompOff must be approved in Workday by the manager within 3 days of the instance of working. CompOff must be used within **30 calendar days** of being earned; unused CompOff lapses after 30 days.

Managers must not routinely require employees to work on holidays or weekly offs; CompOff is for genuine exceptional business needs, not regular weekend work. Patterns of repeated CompOff accumulation are reviewed by HR as part of the well-being oversight process.

## 9. Leave Requests and Administration

All leave requests (except same-day sick and casual leave) must be submitted through Workday. The manager approves or declines within 2 working days of submission. Declined requests must include a written reason, and the manager should propose alternative dates where possible. India employees should maintain a minimum earned leave balance of 5 days at all times to cover unforeseen short-term needs; employees below this threshold for more than 3 consecutive months will be flagged to HR for a well-being check-in.
