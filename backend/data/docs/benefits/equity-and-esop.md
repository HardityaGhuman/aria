# Equity and Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) — GSVH Corp

This document explains how equity grants work at GSVH Corp: the vesting schedule, the cliff, how to exercise options, the tax treatment overview, and what happens to your equity when you leave. It applies to all employees who receive an equity grant as part of their compensation package. Equity grants are governed by the GSVH Corp Equity Incentive Plan (the "Plan") and the individual Grant Agreement; in any conflict between this document and the Grant Agreement, the Grant Agreement governs.

## 1. Why Equity

GSVH Corp grants equity to align employees' long-term financial interest with the company's success. An employee who holds equity benefits when the company grows in value and is motivated by a long-term stake in what they are building, not just the next paycheck. Equity is a complement to base salary and bonus — not a substitute for market-rate cash compensation.

## 2. Grant Types

GSVH Corp grants **Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)** to US employees (subject to IRS eligibility rules) and **Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs)** to non-US employees and to US employees for grants that exceed the ISO annual limit. The tax treatment differs between ISOs and NSOs; consult a tax advisor for your personal situation. This document does not constitute tax advice.

## 3. Vesting Schedule

All equity grants at GSVH Corp follow a **4-year vesting schedule** with a **1-year cliff**:

- **1-year cliff (25%):** No shares vest during the first 12 months from the grant date. On the 1-year anniversary of the grant date, 25% of the total grant vests at once (the "cliff vest").
- **Monthly vesting after the cliff:** Following the cliff, the remaining 75% vests monthly over the next 36 months, at 1/48 of the total grant per month (approximately 2.08% per month of the remaining 75%).

**Example:** An employee receives a grant of 4,800 options at a strike price of $12.00 per share. After 12 months, 1,200 options (25%) vest at the cliff. From month 13 onward, 100 options vest each month (4,800 ÷ 48 = 100). The employee is fully vested at month 48 (year 4).

There is no accelerated vesting on promotion or salary review. Accelerated vesting in a change-of-control event (acquisition) is governed by the Plan and the Grant Agreement.

## 4. Strike Price

The strike price (also called the exercise price) is the price per share at which you may purchase shares by exercising your options. It is set at the Fair Market Value (FMV) of GSVH Corp common stock on the grant date, as determined by a 409A valuation. Your Grant Agreement specifies your strike price; it does not change after the grant date.

The economic value of your options at exercise is the difference between the FMV on the exercise date and your strike price, multiplied by the number of options exercised. Options are only worth exercising when the FMV exceeds the strike price. GSVH Corp conducts 409A valuations at least annually and upon material events (a funding round, a significant acquisition, approaching an IPO).

## 5. Exercising Options

Options may be exercised once vested. To exercise, submit an exercise request through the equity management platform (Carta). You will need to select the exercise type:

- **Cash exercise:** Pay the aggregate strike price in cash for the shares you wish to exercise.
- **Net exercise (cashless exercise):** Available for NSOs; a portion of the shares is withheld to cover the exercise price and tax withholding, and you receive the net shares. This option requires Finance approval and is only available when the company's stock is liquid or in connection with a tender offer or acquisition.

Exercises are processed within 5 business days of a complete, approved request. Shares are reflected in your Carta account after processing.

## 6. Post-Termination Exercise Window

If you leave GSVH Corp for any reason, you have **90 calendar days from your last working day** to exercise any vested options. Options not exercised within this window expire and are returned to the equity pool.

There are two exceptions:
- **Death or permanent disability:** The exercise window extends to 12 months for the estate or beneficiary.
- **Termination for cause:** Vested options may be cancelled immediately upon a for-cause termination, per the Grant Agreement.

The 90-day window is tight. If you are considering leaving, model your exercise decision before your last day — consult the equity management platform for your current vested balance and strike price, and consult a financial advisor or tax professional before exercising, particularly for ISOs.

Unvested options are forfeited on the last working day, regardless of the reason for separation, unless the Grant Agreement specifies otherwise.

## 7. Refresh Grants

GSVH Corp reviews equity balances for high-performing employees annually as part of the compensation cycle. Employees nearing full vesting on their initial grant may receive a refresh grant to extend their equity stake and retain their long-term incentive. Refresh grants follow the same 4-year vesting / 1-year cliff schedule as initial grants and are documented in a new Grant Agreement.

Refresh grants are discretionary and are not guaranteed. They are decided by the compensation committee with input from the employee's manager and are communicated in the annual compensation review.

## 8. Tax Considerations (Overview — Not Tax Advice)

**ISOs (US employees):** ISO exercises do not generate ordinary income tax at exercise (though the spread may trigger Alternative Minimum Tax — AMT). Tax is generally recognised at the time of sale. Holding shares for more than 1 year after exercise and 2 years after the grant date qualifies for long-term capital gains rates.

**NSOs (non-US and above-ISO-limit grants, US):** The spread at exercise (FMV minus strike price) is taxable as ordinary income in the year of exercise. GSVH Corp withholds applicable taxes on net exercises. On a cash exercise, the employee is responsible for setting aside funds to cover the tax liability.

Tax laws change, and this overview is not exhaustive. All employees with equity grants are strongly encouraged to consult a qualified tax advisor before exercising options, particularly around year-end or before a potential liquidity event.

## 9. Confidentiality

Equity grant amounts, strike prices, and vesting balances are confidential. Do not share details of your grant with colleagues outside the context of a legitimate legal or financial consultation. GSVH Corp does not publish individual equity grant sizes; the equity pool and option plan details are disclosed to investors at appropriate times under securities law.

## 10. Changes to the Plan

GSVH Corp reserves the right to amend the Equity Incentive Plan in accordance with the Plan document and applicable law. Material changes that adversely affect vested rights require Board approval and advance notice to affected grantees. Questions about the Plan should be directed to `equity@gsvhcorp.com` or the Finance team.
