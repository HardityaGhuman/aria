# Health and Wellbeing

This document describes the health and wellbeing benefits at Northwind Labs: health insurance, workers' compensation, the employee assistance program, wellness initiatives, and retirement contributions. It applies to all employees, with eligibility for some benefits depending on contract type and scheduled hours. Time away for illness is covered in the leave policies document.

## 1. Health Insurance

The company provides health insurance to eligible employees, with the company covering a substantial share of the premium. Full-time employees on an indefinite contract are eligible from their start date; part-time employees are eligible on a prorated basis once they meet the scheduled-hours threshold described in their contract. You may add eligible dependents to your plan, with the additional premium shared according to the plan terms.

Enrollment happens when you join and again during the annual open-enrollment window, when you can change your plan or coverage. Outside open enrollment, you can change coverage only after a qualifying life event such as marriage, the birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage. Plan details, including what is covered and the provider network, are published in the benefits portal.

## 2. Workers' Compensation

If you are injured or become ill because of your work, workers' compensation provides medical care and wage replacement as required by law, regardless of fault. Report any work-related injury or illness to your manager and HR immediately, however minor it seems, so a claim can be opened and treatment arranged promptly. Prompt reporting protects both your health and your entitlement.

The company maintains the workers' compensation coverage required by law and cooperates fully with claims. Retaliation against an employee for making a legitimate workers' compensation claim is prohibited and is treated as a serious conduct violation.

## 3. Employee Assistance Program

The company offers a confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP) to support your mental health and personal wellbeing. The EAP provides free, confidential counseling and practical support for issues such as stress, anxiety, grief, relationship difficulties, financial worries, and legal questions. You and your immediate household can use the service.

The EAP is independent and confidential: the company receives only aggregate, anonymized usage data and never learns who used the service or why. You can contact the EAP directly at any time through the details in the benefits portal — you do not need manager or HR approval to use it.

## 4. Wellness Initiatives

We invest in everyday wellbeing through initiatives that may include a wellness allowance toward gym membership or fitness activities, regular health check campaigns, ergonomic assessments for your workspace, and mental-health awareness training for managers. Specific offerings vary by location and are listed in the benefits portal.

Wellbeing is also a matter of how we work. Managers are expected to support reasonable workloads, encourage people to take their PTO, and respect time outside working hours. If workload or stress is affecting your health, raise it with your manager or HR early.

## 5. Retirement Contributions

The company supports your long-term financial wellbeing through a retirement savings plan with company contributions. Eligible employees can contribute from their salary through payroll, and the company matches contributions up to the percentage set out in the plan documents. Contributions and any match are shown on your payslip and in the benefits portal.

You choose your contribution level and can change it during the periods the plan allows. The retirement plan is administered by an external provider; consult the plan documents or a qualified financial adviser for decisions about your own savings, as the company cannot give individual financial advice.

## 6. Eligibility and Changes

Benefit eligibility depends on your contract type and scheduled hours, as summarized in your offer and contract. When your circumstances change — hours, dependents, or a qualifying life event — update your details in the benefits portal promptly, because some changes have time limits. If a benefit interacts with pay, such as a premium deduction, the change flows through to payroll in the next cycle. Questions about eligibility or enrollment go to HR or the benefits administrator.
