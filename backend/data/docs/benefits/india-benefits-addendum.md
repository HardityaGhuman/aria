# India Benefits Addendum — GSVH India Pvt Ltd

This document describes the benefits provided to employees of GSVH India Pvt Ltd beyond statutory requirements. It supplements the global benefits policies (`benefits/health-and-wellbeing.md` and `benefits/remote-work.md`). Where this addendum differs from the global policy, this addendum governs for India employees.

## 1. Group Medical Insurance

All full-time and part-time (20+ hours/week) employees of GSVH India Pvt Ltd are enrolled in the company group medical insurance policy on their date of joining. Coverage begins on Day 1; there is no waiting period for pre-existing conditions (subject to the policy terms).

**Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) per insured life per policy year.** The policy year runs 1 April to 31 March, aligned to the Indian financial year.

Coverage includes:
- In-patient hospitalization (room rent up to single AC room, or the sub-limit in the policy schedule).
- Pre-hospitalization expenses up to 30 days before admission.
- Post-hospitalization expenses up to 60 days after discharge.
- Day-care procedures that do not require an overnight stay.
- Domiciliary hospitalization for conditions where hospitalization is not possible.
- Ambulance charges up to ₹3,000 per event.

The policy includes a **cashless facility** at network hospitals. Employees receive a policy booklet and network hospital list via the insurance portal. For non-network or emergency hospitalization, the reimbursement claim process applies; submit claims with original documents through HR within 30 days of discharge.

### Dependent Coverage

Employees may enrol their spouse and up to 2 children (up to age 25 if in full-time education) as dependents under the group policy. The **company subsidises dependent premiums at 50%** of the incremental premium; the remaining 50% is deducted from the employee's salary in the April payroll. Enrolment changes (adding a spouse after marriage, adding a newborn) must be communicated to HR Payroll within 30 days of the life event.

Parents and in-laws may be added as optional dependents at the employee's own cost; HR provides the premium schedule annually.

## 2. Term Life Insurance

GSVH India Pvt Ltd provides a group term life insurance policy covering all permanent employees. The sum insured is **3 times the employee's annual CTC**, rounded to the nearest ₹1,00,000. Coverage is in force from the date of joining.

In the event of the insured employee's death during the policy term, the sum insured is paid to the nominated beneficiary. Employees must nominate a beneficiary in Workday within 30 days of joining; an unmarried employee with no nomination on record defaults to the legal heir.

The policy also includes an accidental death and permanent total disability rider at **1 times the annual CTC**, payable in addition to the base sum insured in the event of accidental death, and as a lump sum in the event of permanent total disability.

Premiums for term life and accidental cover are paid entirely by GSVH India Pvt Ltd; there is no employee contribution. The premium paid by the company is a perquisite and is included in gross salary for TDS computation.

## 3. Personal Accident Insurance

In addition to the accidental rider in the term life policy, employees are covered under a standalone group personal accident (GPA) policy for partial permanent disability and temporary total disability (TTD), providing a proportional payout per the benefit schedule. The GPA policy covers accidents 24 hours a day, worldwide, whether on or off duty. Policy details are available from HR.

## 4. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

GSVH India Pvt Ltd provides access to the global EAP described in `benefits/health-and-wellbeing.md`. In India, the EAP is delivered through a local provider offering:
- Confidential counselling (up to 6 sessions per issue per year at no cost to the employee).
- Legal and financial advisory helpline.
- Mental health and stress management resources in English, Hindi, and Kannada.
- Manager helpline for guidance on handling team mental-health concerns.

The EAP helpline number is published on the HR intranet page and in the welcome email. Employees may call anonymously; the company receives only aggregate, anonymised utilization data.

## 5. Provident Fund and Gratuity

EPF and gratuity are statutory and are fully described in `finance/india-payroll-addendum.md` and `hr/india-employment-addendum.md`. These are not discretionary benefits — they are employer obligations under Indian law.

## 6. Meal Benefits

GSVH India Pvt Ltd provides a monthly meal allowance of **₹2,200 per month** (the maximum non-taxable meal allowance as per prevailing Income Tax rules) through a meal card or as a designated CTC component, as specified in the employee's compensation letter. This is a tax-optimized component; employees should ensure their meal allowance is properly declared in their investment declaration to avail the tax benefit.

The Bengaluru office also provides subsidized lunch at the office cafeteria on days when the employee is working from the office. The subsidy is not convertible to cash if the employee works from home on a given day.

## 7. Home-Office Stipend (India)

Eligible remote and hybrid employees in India receive a one-time home-office equipment stipend equivalent to **₹35,000**, reimbursed against receipts through the expense claim process in Workday. The global stipend ($500 USD) converts at the prevailing rate; ₹35,000 is the fixed India amount and is not adjusted for exchange rate movements during the policy year.

Eligible items are the same as the global policy: ergonomic chair, desk, monitor, keyboard, mouse, or equivalent workspace equipment. The stipend is a one-time benefit per employment; employees who have previously claimed it are not eligible for a repeat claim on re-joining.

## 8. Relocation Assistance

Employees relocated to a different city at GSVH India Pvt Ltd's request receive relocation assistance per the terms in their offer or relocation letter, including:
- Reimbursement of packers and movers costs up to ₹75,000 against receipts.
- Temporary accommodation for up to 30 days at a company-approved service apartment or hotel.
- A one-time settling-in allowance of ₹15,000 (taxable) paid in the month of relocation.

Employees who relocate voluntarily (self-initiated) are not eligible for relocation assistance unless specifically agreed in writing with HR and Finance before the move.

## 9. Benefits Changes and Queries

Benefits are reviewed annually, typically in Q4, for renewal and adjustment. Material changes to the group medical or term life policy are communicated to employees at least 30 days before the new policy year begins. For benefits queries, contact `hr-india@gsvhcorp.com` or raise a ticket in the HR service portal.
