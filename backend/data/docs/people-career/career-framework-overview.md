# Career Framework Overview — GSVH Corp

This document explains GSVH Corp's career framework: the level structure, the criteria for each level, the promotion process, and how the framework connects to compensation. It applies to all employees globally. The engineering-specific competency matrix is tabulated in `people-career/engineering-leveling-matrix.csv`; this document is the prose companion that explains the logic behind the levels.

## 1. Why a Career Framework

Without a clear framework, promotion decisions become opaque — dependent on who your manager is and how visible your work happens to be. A career framework makes expectations explicit: here is what GSVH Corp needs to see at each level, and here is the concrete path from one level to the next. It reduces bias, improves the quality of performance conversations, and makes the company's compensation structure defensible.

This framework is a guide, not a rigid checklist. Growth at GSVH Corp is about sustained, demonstrated impact — not about ticking boxes.

## 2. Level Structure

GSVH Corp uses a **7-level framework (L1–L7)**. Each level applies globally across departments; the specific competencies for a given discipline (engineering, finance, operations, HR) are described in the relevant leveling matrix or role profile.

| Level | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **L1** | Associate | Entry level. Learning the role, completing well-defined tasks with close guidance. |
| **L2** | Junior | Developing proficiency. Works independently on defined tasks; contributes to team work with some guidance. |
| **L3** | Mid-Level | Fully independent contributor. Owns defined work end-to-end; beginning to mentor or guide L1–L2 colleagues. |
| **L4** | Senior | Broad scope, high autonomy. Leads complex projects; influences peers; consistently delivers above the bar. |
| **L5** | Staff / Lead | Cross-team impact. Sets technical or functional direction for a domain; a force-multiplier for others. |
| **L6** | Principal | Company-wide or cross-organizational impact. Defines strategy in a functional area; rare; requires sustained extraordinary impact. |
| **L7** | Distinguished / VP+ | Executive or near-executive scope. Shapes GSVH Corp's direction in a major domain. Typically reached through a combination of technical mastery, business acumen, and leadership at scale. |

Most career growth at GSVH Corp happens in the L1–L5 range. L6 and L7 represent roles and impacts that affect the company at a strategic level; there are few such positions, and readiness at those levels is assessed by the executive team.

## 3. Level Criteria (All Disciplines)

Each level is defined along four dimensions. The discipline-specific matrices (e.g., `people-career/engineering-leveling-matrix.csv`) provide concrete behavioural indicators; these cross-cutting criteria apply to all roles.

### Scope and Impact

- **L1–L2:** Impact within a task or sub-component of a project.
- **L3:** Impact across a complete project or work stream.
- **L4:** Impact across multiple projects or a team-wide domain.
- **L5:** Cross-team or cross-department impact; work influences how other teams operate.
- **L6–L7:** Division-wide or company-wide impact; sets direction that others execute.

### Autonomy and Ownership

- **L1–L2:** Requires regular guidance; asks before acting on ambiguous situations.
- **L3:** Works independently on clear problems; escalates appropriately.
- **L4:** Operates with high autonomy; defines their own priorities within team goals; escalates rarely.
- **L5:** Self-directing; defines the problems worth solving in their domain; brings others along.
- **L6–L7:** Defines the strategy; others look to them for direction.

### Collaboration and Influence

- **L1–L2:** Works well within the immediate team.
- **L3:** Builds effective working relationships inside and outside the team.
- **L4:** Influential with peers; communicates clearly to non-experts; beginning to lead cross-functional collaboration.
- **L5:** Significant influence across teams; shapes decisions made by peers and managers alike.
- **L6–L7:** Influences the executive team and external stakeholders; represents GSVH Corp externally.

### Craft and Expertise

- **L1–L2:** Building foundational skills; relies on established patterns.
- **L3:** Solid competence in the core skills of the discipline; identifies improvements.
- **L4:** Deep expertise in primary areas; breadth across adjacent areas; raises the bar for the team.
- **L5:** Recognised expert; creates new practices or standards adopted beyond the team.
- **L6–L7:** Thought leadership in the field; contributes to the external professional community.

## 4. Promotion Process

Promotions at GSVH Corp are assessed on a **semi-annual cadence**, aligned to the performance review cycle: in June (for the first half of the year) and December (for the full year). Off-cycle promotions are rare and require CHRO approval.

### Process steps

1. **Manager nominates:** The manager submits a promotion nomination in Workday during the calibration window (typically 4 weeks before the cycle closes). The nomination includes: current level, proposed level, and a written case for promotion citing specific evidence mapped to the level criteria.
2. **Calibration:** The manager presents the nomination in the semi-annual calibration session alongside all other nominations in the department. The calibration panel (manager, skip-level, HR Business Partner, and one cross-functional peer) ensures nominations are assessed consistently across the team, not in isolation.
3. **HR and Finance review:** HR confirms the promotion is within the headcount plan and budget. Finance confirms the new salary sits within the approved compensation band for the proposed level.
4. **Employee is notified:** After approval, the manager delivers the promotion conversation before any written communication. HR issues the promotion letter in Workday.

### What the calibration is looking for

The calibration panel assesses whether the employee is **already operating at the next level**, not whether they will be able to when promoted. "Potential" alone does not justify promotion; GSVH Corp promotes people who have demonstrated the next level's impact over a sustained period, typically at least 6 consecutive months. A strong performance review in one cycle followed by regression is not sufficient evidence.

The one exception is the L1→L2 transition for early-career employees, which can occur on a shorter cycle (3 months) when rapid demonstrated growth clearly supports it.

## 5. Compensation at Each Level

Each level has an approved compensation band defined by Finance and HR, based on annual market benchmarking. The band has a minimum, midpoint, and maximum. Promotion to a new level brings the employee's base salary to at least the minimum of the new band; where the employee is already above the new band minimum, the promotion may be accompanied by a market adjustment or a merit increase, per the compensation committee's decision.

Band details are confidential to HR and Finance. Employees who believe their compensation is not consistent with their level and contribution should raise this with their manager or HR Business Partner.

For reference, `finance/salary-bands.csv` (HR-access only) tabulates the approved band ranges by level and region.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I skip a level?** In rare cases, yes — an exceptionally high-impact new hire may be brought in at L4 or L5 directly. Internal promotions skipping a level are theoretically possible but have a very high bar; they require the same evidence of sustained performance at the skip-to level, plus evidence that the in-between level would have been transient.

**What if my manager disagrees with my self-assessment?** Calibration is the right place to resolve disagreements. If you believe your calibration outcome was unfair, discuss it with your manager first, then with the HR Business Partner. The promotion process is designed to be visible enough that single-manager bias is surface-able.

**Does GSVH Corp have a "performance out" for people who stay at a level too long?** No — staying at the same level is not itself a problem. GSVH Corp does not have a mandatory promotion timeline or a "grow or go" policy for individual contributors. Performance issues are addressed through the PIP process (`hr/performance-improvement-plan.md`), not through arbitrary level expectations.
