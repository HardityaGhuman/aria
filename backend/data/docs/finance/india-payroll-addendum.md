# India Payroll Addendum — GSVH India Pvt Ltd

This document supplements the global GSVH Corp compensation and payroll policy (`finance/compensation-and-payroll.md`) with the specific payroll rules, deductions, statutory contributions, and tax mechanics that apply to employees of GSVH India Pvt Ltd. Where this addendum differs from the global policy, this addendum governs for India employees.

## 1. Payroll Cycle

India employees are paid on a **monthly cycle**. Salary is credited to the employee's registered bank account on or before the **last business day of each calendar month** for that month's service. The payroll cutoff for attendance, variable pay, and reimbursements is the **25th of the month**. Any inputs received after the 25th are included in the following month's payroll.

Payslips are generated and published in the Workday self-service portal by the 1st of the following month. Employees should review their payslip within 5 business days and raise any discrepancy with HR Payroll at `payroll-india@gsvhcorp.com` immediately. Corrections are processed in the next payroll cycle unless the error materially affects the employee's financial position, in which case Finance targets a correction within 3 business days.

## 2. Salary Structure

India salaries are structured with a breakdown of components that affects statutory deductions and tax treatment. A typical cost-to-company (CTC) structure at GSVH India Pvt Ltd includes:

- **Basic Salary** — typically 40–50% of CTC. Forms the basis for EPF contribution, gratuity calculation, and certain tax exemptions.
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)** — partially exempt from income tax subject to conditions (actual HRA received, 40% or 50% of basic depending on metro/non-metro, actual rent paid minus 10% of basic). Employees must submit rent receipts annually.
- **Special Allowance** — fully taxable; makes up the remainder of CTC after other components.
- **Performance Bonus** — discretionary, based on company and individual performance; processed as a separate payroll component in the month paid.

Salary components are specified in the employee's offer letter and compensation letter. Changes to the CTC structure require HR and Finance approval and are communicated in a revised compensation letter.

## 3. Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)

EPF contributions apply to all India employees where the gross salary is at or below the statutory threshold (currently ₹15,000/month basic, but GSVH India Pvt Ltd contributes on actual basic for all employees as part of its standard CTC structure).

- **Employee contribution:** **12%** of basic salary, deducted from gross pay each month.
- **Employer contribution:** **12%** of basic salary, paid additionally by GSVH India Pvt Ltd on top of CTC. Of the employer's 12%: 8.33% is remitted to the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) subject to the statutory wage ceiling, and the remainder to the EPF account.

Contributions are remitted to the EPFO by the **15th of the following month**. Employees' Universal Account Number (UAN) is generated at joining and must be self-verified by the employee in the EPFO portal. The UAN is portable across employers and stays with the employee throughout their career.

Employees who wish to make voluntary contributions above the mandatory 12% (Voluntary Provident Fund, VPF) may do so through a written request to HR Payroll; VPF contributions attract the same interest rate as EPF.

## 4. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)

GSVH India Pvt Ltd deducts TDS from salary each month under Section 192 of the Income Tax Act 1961. The TDS amount is based on projected annual income and applicable deductions, spread equally across the 12 months of the financial year.

### Tax Slabs (FY 2026–27, New Tax Regime)

The new tax regime (default from FY 2023-24 onwards unless employee opts for the old regime) applies the following slabs:

| Annual Income | Tax Rate |
|---------------|----------|
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹3,00,001 – ₹7,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹7,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹10,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹12,00,001 – ₹15,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹15,00,000 | 30% |

A standard deduction of ₹75,000 is available under the new regime. The 87A rebate applies for income up to ₹7,00,000 under the new regime, making effective tax nil at that threshold.

Employees opting for the old regime must declare this at the start of the financial year; the old regime permits deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D, HRA, etc.) but has higher slab rates.

### Investment Declarations

Employees must submit their investment declaration using the HR Payroll portal by **15 April** of the new financial year (for the year beginning that April). Supporting documents (insurance premium receipts, home loan statements, rent receipts) must be submitted by **15 February**. GSVH India Pvt Ltd computes TDS based on declared investments; actual proof must match declarations. Failure to submit proof results in TDS reversal (the previously exempt amount becomes taxable and is recovered in the February–March payroll runs).

## 5. Gratuity

Gratuity is a statutory benefit under the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, applicable after **5 continuous years of service**. The formula:

**Gratuity = (Last drawn basic salary × 15 × completed years of service) ÷ 26**

The divisor of 26 represents the working days in a month for this calculation. "Last drawn basic salary" means the basic salary and dearness allowance (DA) as on the date of separation, excluding other allowances. Gratuity is tax-exempt up to statutory limits; amounts above the exemption threshold are taxable.

GSVH India Pvt Ltd funds the gratuity obligation through a group gratuity policy with an approved life insurer. No amount is deducted from the employee's salary for gratuity — it is entirely an employer cost.

## 6. Professional Tax (PT)

Professional Tax is levied by the state government and varies by state. GSVH India Pvt Ltd deducts PT from salary per the applicable state slab and remits it to the state authority within the statutory deadline. The deduction appears on the payslip as "Professional Tax". PT paid is deductible as a deduction from salary income for income tax purposes.

## 7. Other Deductions

The following may appear as deductions on the India payslip:

- **ESI (Employees' State Insurance):** Applicable for employees with gross salary at or below the statutory threshold. Employee contribution is 0.75% of gross salary; employer contribution is 3.25%. Employees above the threshold are covered under the company group medical policy and are not subject to ESI deduction.
- **LWF (Labour Welfare Fund):** Small statutory deduction per the applicable state LWF Act; typically a few hundred rupees per year.
- **Salary Advance Recovery:** If the employee has taken a salary advance, recovery is shown as a separate line item.

## 8. Full and Final Settlement

On separation from GSVH India Pvt Ltd, Finance processes a Full and Final (F&F) settlement that includes:
- Salary earned through the last working day.
- Encashment of accrued earned leave (as per `time-and-leave/india-leave-addendum.md`).
- Gratuity (if eligible — 5 completed years of service).
- Any outstanding reimbursements.
- Recovery of any salary advances, notice period shortfall (if employee served less than the 60-day notice), or excess leave.

F&F is targeted to be processed within **45 days** of the last working day. Finance and HR jointly issue a No Dues Certificate and release Form 16 for the partial financial year in the subsequent Form 16 cycle. The EPF withdrawal or transfer is initiated by the employee directly on the EPFO portal using the UAN.
