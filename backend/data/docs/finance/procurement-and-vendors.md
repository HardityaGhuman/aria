# Procurement and Vendor Management — GSVH Corp

This document defines GSVH Corp's procurement policy: when a purchase order is required, the vendor onboarding process, the approval tier thresholds, vendor due diligence requirements, and contract management. It applies to all managers and budget holders who engage external vendors, suppliers, or service providers. Individual employee expense claims are governed by `finance/expenses-and-reimbursement.md`.

## 1. Purchase Order Requirement

A **Purchase Order (PO) is required for any single vendor transaction or contract valued at $1,000 or more** (USD, or local currency equivalent at the prevailing rate). Transactions below $1,000 may be processed directly via expense claim or company card where the vendor does not issue a formal contract, but they are still subject to the approval limits in Section 3.

Attempting to structure a purchase as multiple transactions each below $1,000 to avoid the PO threshold is prohibited and constitutes a conduct violation. Finance reviews purchase patterns for structuring.

All SaaS subscriptions, professional services engagements, consulting agreements, and any recurring vendor relationship expected to exceed $1,000 in a 12-month period must be set up with a PO and a signed contract, regardless of the individual invoice size.

## 2. Vendor Onboarding

Before a new vendor can receive a PO or be paid by GSVH Corp, they must complete vendor onboarding. The SLA for standard vendor onboarding is **5 business days** from the date the vendor submits a complete onboarding pack. Finance initiates onboarding; the requesting manager is responsible for ensuring the vendor submits the pack promptly.

The onboarding pack includes:
- Vendor registration form (business name, address, tax registration number).
- Bank account details for payment.
- W-9 (US vendors) or equivalent tax form (non-US vendors).
- Certificate of insurance where required by the contract type.
- Signed GSVH Corp Vendor Code of Conduct acknowledgement.
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA) if the vendor will process personal data on GSVH Corp's behalf.

IT Security reviews any vendor with system access or data-handling responsibilities; this review runs in parallel with Finance onboarding and must be complete before the contract is signed. An incomplete IT Security review does not pause the Finance onboarding clock but does block contract execution.

Finance maintains a Preferred Vendor List for common categories (travel, software, office supplies, temporary staffing). Managers are expected to use preferred vendors where available; using a non-preferred vendor for a category covered by the list requires department-head approval and a brief written justification.

## 3. Approval Tiers

All vendor spend requires approval at the appropriate tier before a PO is issued or a contract is signed. Approval is based on the **total contract value (TCV)** — the full committed value over the contract term, not the annual or monthly cost.

| TCV | Required Approval |
|-----|-------------------|
| Up to $1,000 | Manager (expense claim path) |
| $1,001 – $10,000 | Manager + Department Head |
| $10,001 – $50,000 | Manager + Department Head + VP of Finance |
| Above $50,000 | Manager + Department Head + VP of Finance + CFO |

Renewals are treated as new contracts for approval purposes — a $12,000/year SaaS tool requires the same approvals on renewal as it did at initial purchase. Multi-year contracts are approved at the full TCV; a 3-year contract at $20,000/year requires CFO approval.

No purchase commitment (verbal or written) may be made to a vendor before the appropriate approval is in place. Finance will not issue a PO for a commitment already made without approval; the manager assumes personal liability for unapproved commitments.

## 4. Contract Review and Signing

All vendor contracts must be reviewed by Finance (for commercial terms) before signing. Contracts with a TCV above $50,000, or any contract that includes an indemnification clause, IP assignment, or data processing obligations, must also be reviewed by Legal before signing. Legal review SLA is 5 business days for standard contracts and 10 business days for complex or custom agreements.

Signing authority mirrors the approval tiers: contracts with a TCV above $50,000 must be signed by the CFO or CEO. Department heads may sign contracts up to $50,000 TCV. Managers may not sign vendor contracts unless specifically granted signing authority in writing by Finance.

Never sign a vendor's standard terms without Legal review — vendor terms are written to protect the vendor, not GSVH Corp.

## 5. Preferred Payment Terms

GSVH Corp's standard payment term is **Net 30** from the date of an approved invoice. Finance negotiates terms with high-volume vendors; the Preferred Vendor List notes the agreed terms for each. Early-payment discounts (e.g., 2/10 Net 30) must be flagged to Finance immediately, as they require accelerated approval to capture the discount.

Payments are processed in the weekly AP run. Invoices received and approved by Monday noon are paid in that week's run. Invoices must reference the PO number; Finance will return invoices without a PO reference to the requesting manager.

## 6. Vendor Due Diligence

Finance and Legal assess each new vendor for the following risks before onboarding is complete:
- **Financial stability:** for strategic or sole-source vendors, Finance may request recent financial statements or credit references.
- **Sanctions screening:** all new vendors are screened against OFAC and other applicable sanctions lists before the first payment.
- **Conflicts of interest:** the requesting manager must disclose any personal relationship with the vendor or its principals on the vendor onboarding form. Non-disclosure is a conduct violation.
- **Data protection:** any vendor handling personal data is subject to DPA execution and a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) coordinated by Legal and IT.

High-criticality vendors (sole-source, infrastructure, or those handling restricted data) are reviewed annually. The vendor management log is maintained by Finance and is available to department heads on request.

## 7. Vendor Performance and Offboarding

Managers are responsible for monitoring vendor performance against contract SLAs and escalating underperformance to Finance before the next renewal. Finance maintains a vendor performance log and shares it with the Preferred Vendor List committee annually.

When a vendor relationship ends, Finance closes the PO, notifies IT to revoke any system access (within 24 hours of contract end), and ensures the final invoice reconciles to the contract value. Any vendor equipment on GSVH Corp premises must be returned within 10 business days of contract end.

## 8. Company Card Policy

Company cards are issued to employees in roles with regular business-spend requirements. Company card charges are subject to the same approval thresholds as expense claims but are posted directly to the company ledger. Cardholders must reconcile their card statement in the expense portal within 7 days of each monthly statement close. Unreconciled charges are escalated to the cardholder's manager. Personal charges on company cards must be repaid within 30 days of the statement; repeated personal charges are grounds for card revocation and disciplinary action.
