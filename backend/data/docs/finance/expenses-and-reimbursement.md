# Expenses and Reimbursement

This document explains how to claim business expenses at Northwind Labs: what is reimbursable, the approval limits, travel and meals, the company car program, parking, and tuition and relocation reimbursement. It applies to all employees who incur costs on the company's behalf. Compensation and payroll are covered in a separate finance document.

## 1. General Principles

The company reimburses reasonable, necessary expenses you incur while doing your job. An expense is reimbursable when it is business-related, supported by a receipt, and within policy. When in doubt, ask before you spend. Personal expenses, fines, and costs without a receipt are not reimbursable.

Submit expense claims using form **FIN-EXP-014** through the expense portal within 30 days of incurring the cost. Attach an itemized receipt for every line. Claims submitted more than 60 days late may be refused. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## 2. Approval Limits

Approval follows the amount of the claim. Expenses up to $200 are approved by your direct manager. Expenses from $200 to $2,000 require department-head approval. Expenses above $2,000 require Finance approval in addition to the department head. No one may approve their own expense claim; a claim by a manager is approved one level up.

Splitting a single purchase into smaller claims to stay under an approval threshold is prohibited and is treated as a conduct issue. Pre-approval is required for any single planned expense expected to exceed $2,000.

## 3. Travel and Meals

Book business travel through the company travel tool where possible, choosing economy fares for flights under six hours. Hotels should be of a standard, business-grade category; choose a reasonable option rather than the cheapest or the most expensive. The company reimburses actual, receipted travel costs rather than paying a flat per diem, unless a per diem is specified for a particular trip.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $60 per day against receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at a pre-approved client event. Reasonable ground transport — taxis, rideshare, public transit, or mileage in a personal car — is reimbursable; personal-car mileage is paid at the published per-mile rate. Keep travel bookings flexible only where the business case justifies the higher fare.

## 4. Company Car

Roles that require frequent travel may be eligible for a company car, as designated by the department head and Finance. The company car is for business use, with incidental personal use permitted. Fuel and routine maintenance for business use are covered through the company fuel card; keep fuel receipts.

Drivers must hold a valid license, follow all traffic laws, and report any accident or damage immediately to Finance and facilities. Traffic fines are the driver's personal responsibility and are not reimbursable. A company car must be returned in good condition when the role no longer requires it or when employment ends; see the IT and facilities equipment-return process for coordination.

## 5. Parking

The company provides a limited number of on-site parking spaces, allocated first to company-car drivers and to employees with accessibility needs, then by request to facilities. Where on-site parking is unavailable, the company reimburses reasonable paid parking for business trips against receipts. Routine commuting parking is not reimbursable except where offered as a specific benefit; see the benefits remote-work and commuting policy for any commuter benefit.

## 6. Tuition Reimbursement

The company supports professional development through tuition reimbursement for courses, certifications, and degrees relevant to your role or career path at the company. Reimbursement requires manager and department-head pre-approval and is capped at $3,000 per calendar year. The company reimburses tuition and required materials on successful completion — typically a passing grade or a completed certification — against receipts.

If you leave the company within 12 months of receiving tuition reimbursement, a prorated portion may be repayable, as set out in the reimbursement agreement you sign before the course. This protects the company's investment while still encouraging you to develop.

## 7. Relocation Reimbursement

When the company asks you to relocate for a role, or hires you into a role that requires relocation, it may reimburse reasonable moving costs up to a cap agreed in your offer or relocation letter. Eligible costs typically include professional movers, transport, and temporary accommodation for a defined period. As with tuition, a prorated repayment may apply if you leave within 12 months, per the relocation agreement.

## 8. Fraud and Audit

Expense claims are subject to audit by Finance. Submitting a false or inflated claim is fraud and is grounds for termination and possible legal action. Finance samples claims for review and may ask for additional documentation. Approvers are accountable for the claims they approve, so review each claim against policy before approving it rather than approving on trust alone.
