# India Employment Addendum — GSVH India Pvt Ltd

This addendum applies to all employees employed by GSVH India Pvt Ltd, the Indian subsidiary of GSVH Corp. Where this addendum differs from the global GSVH Corp policies, this addendum governs for India-based employees. Where this addendum is silent, the global policy applies.

## 1. Employing Entity

Your employer is **GSVH India Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Employment is governed by the applicable Indian labour laws including the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, the Payment of Wages Act, the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952, the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, and relevant state-specific shop and establishment acts.

## 2. Working Hours and the Working Week

The standard working week is **40 hours**, Monday to Friday, consistent with the global GSVH Corp policy. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST; flexible start and finish times are available by agreement with your manager. Overtime beyond 40 hours requires prior manager approval and is compensated in accordance with applicable Indian labour law.

For employees covered by the Shops and Establishments Act of the relevant state, mandatory rest periods and maximum daily working hours are observed as required by law, which in all cases satisfies or exceeds global policy minimums.

## 3. Notice Period

The **notice period for India-based employees is 60 calendar days**, on both the employee's side and GSVH India Pvt Ltd's side, regardless of seniority level. This supersedes the global policy notice period for India employees. Notice must be given in writing. During the notice period, the employee continues to work and receive full pay unless a payment in lieu of notice (PILON) is agreed in writing.

Employees in their 90-day probation period serve a shorter notice of 2 weeks on either side, as stated in the employment contract.

## 4. Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)

GSVH India Pvt Ltd participates in the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) scheme as required by the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952. Both the employee and GSVH India Pvt Ltd contribute **12% of the employee's basic salary** per month to the EPF account. Contributions are remitted to the EPFO by the 15th of the following month.

The employee's 12% contribution goes entirely to the Provident Fund. Of the employer's 12% contribution, 8.33% is diverted to the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) up to the statutory wage ceiling, and the remainder goes to the PF. Employees can view their PF balance and UAN through the EPFO portal. For employees earning above the statutory wage ceiling, contributions may be made on the full salary or the capped salary per the applicable election; HR Payroll will confirm the structure for your salary band at joining.

## 5. Gratuity

Employees who have completed **5 continuous years of service** with GSVH India Pvt Ltd are entitled to gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972. The gratuity formula is:

**Gratuity = (Last drawn basic salary × 15 days × completed years of service) ÷ 26**

The divisor of 26 represents the number of working days in a month for the purpose of the gratuity calculation. "Last drawn basic salary" includes dearness allowance but excludes other allowances, bonuses, and commissions. Gratuity is payable on resignation, retirement, termination, death, or disablement. It is paid within 30 days of the entitlement arising.

**Example:** An employee with a last drawn basic salary of ₹80,000/month and 7 completed years of service receives: (80,000 × 15 × 7) ÷ 26 = ₹3,23,077.

GSVH India Pvt Ltd maintains a group gratuity policy with an approved insurer to fund this obligation. The scheme details are available from HR Payroll.

## 6. Income Tax and TDS

GSVH India Pvt Ltd deducts Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) from salary each month in accordance with the Income Tax Act 1961 and the employee's applicable tax slab. Employees submit their investment declarations at the start of the financial year (April) and provide supporting documents by February of the following year. Failure to submit declarations results in TDS computed at the maximum applicable rate without deductions.

The financial year for payroll purposes runs **1 April to 31 March**. Form 16 is issued by 15 June each year for the preceding financial year. Employees are responsible for their own personal tax filings; GSVH India Pvt Ltd deducts the correct TDS based on declarations provided but cannot give individual tax advice. Consult a qualified chartered accountant for personal tax planning.

## 7. Payroll Cycle

India employees are paid on a **monthly cycle**, with salary credited to the employee's bank account on or before the last business day of each month. The payroll cutoff for attendance and variable inputs is the 25th of each month. Payslips are available in Workday by the 1st of the following month.

New joiners who start after the 15th of a month receive a prorated salary for the partial month in the following month's payroll.

## 8. Leaves and Public Holidays

India-specific leave entitlements are described in `time-and-leave/india-leave-addendum.md`. Statutory public holidays (national and state-specific) are published annually in the GSVH India Pvt Ltd holiday calendar on the intranet. India employees observe both the applicable statutory holidays and any additional GSVH India Pvt Ltd discretionary holidays as published each calendar year.

## 9. Statutory Benefits

In addition to EPF and gratuity, GSVH India Pvt Ltd provides the following statutory benefits where applicable:

- **Employees' State Insurance (ESI):** Applicable for employees with gross salary up to the statutory threshold. Both employer and employee contribute per the current ESI rate schedule. Employees above the threshold are covered under the company group medical policy instead.
- **Professional Tax (PT):** Deducted from salary per the applicable state schedule and remitted to the state government.
- **Group Medical Insurance:** All India employees are covered under a group medical policy; see `benefits/india-benefits-addendum.md` for sum insured and coverage details.

## 10. Standing Orders and Grievances

GSVH India Pvt Ltd operates under Certified Standing Orders (or Model Standing Orders, as applicable) for the relevant establishments. Employees may raise grievances through the HR grievance mechanism described in `legal-compliance/anti-harassment-and-grievance.md`. The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) constituted under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH) Act 2013 is operational at all GSVH India Pvt Ltd establishments with 10 or more employees.

Contact HR Bengaluru for the ICC composition and for any POSH-related queries.
