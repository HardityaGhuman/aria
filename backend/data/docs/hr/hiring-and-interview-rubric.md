# Hiring and Interview Rubric — GSVH Corp

This document is for hiring managers and interviewers at GSVH Corp. It defines the 4-stage interview loop, the scorecard and rating scale, the debrief process, and the offer approval chain. Following this rubric consistently ensures every candidate receives a fair, structured evaluation and protects GSVH Corp from bias-related legal exposure.

## 1. The 4-Stage Interview Loop

All full-time hires at GSVH Corp go through a **4-stage interview loop**. The stages are designed so each one answers a distinct question about the candidate.

### Stage 1: Recruiter Screen (30 minutes, phone/video)

Conducted by a Talent Acquisition team member. Objectives: confirm the candidate's motivation and availability, verify headline qualifications against the job description, explain the role and GSVH Corp's business, and assess basic communication skills. The recruiter documents a brief pass/no-pass recommendation in the ATS. Candidates who pass move to Stage 2 within 5 business days.

### Stage 2: Hiring Manager Screen (45–60 minutes, video)

The hiring manager assesses role fit in depth: relevant experience, approach to problem-solving, and whether the candidate's career goals align with what the role offers. The manager also introduces the team's working style and answers questions honestly. A structured scorecard (Section 3) is completed within 24 hours.

### Stage 3: Technical or Functional Assessment (60–90 minutes, varies by role)

The format depends on the discipline: a live coding session and system design discussion for engineers; a case study or analytical exercise for finance and operations; a work sample review for design and product roles. Assessors must use the pre-agreed rubric for the role — no improvised questions. At least two interviewers participate so no single person's bias determines the outcome. Scorecards are completed within 24 hours.

### Stage 4: Culture and Values Interview (45 minutes, video, cross-functional interviewer)

Conducted by a peer or cross-functional interviewer outside the candidate's direct team. This stage assesses alignment with GSVH Corp's values and collaboration style using structured behavioural questions (STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result). The interviewer is trained and certified before running this stage independently. Scorecard completed within 24 hours.

## 2. Scheduling and Candidate Experience

The full loop should be completed within **10 business days** of the Stage 1 pass. Recruiter coordinates all scheduling centrally. Interviewers must honour scheduled slots; last-minute reschedules require the recruiter's approval and a same-day candidate notification with an apology. Candidates are given the names and roles of their interviewers before each stage.

After the final stage, the candidate receives a status update within 3 business days. No candidate should be left waiting more than 3 business days after their last interview without an update from the recruiter.

## 3. Scorecard and Rating Scale

Every interviewer completes a scorecard in the ATS (Greenhouse) within **24 hours of the interview**. The scorecard uses a **1–4 rating scale** on each dimension:

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 4 | Strong Yes | Clearly meets or exceeds the bar; would strongly advocate for this hire. |
| 3 | Yes | Meets the bar with no significant concerns. |
| 2 | No | Does not clearly meet the bar; concerns outweigh positives. |
| 1 | Strong No | Significant gaps or red flags; would not hire regardless of other feedback. |

Interviewers must score each dimension independently before reading others' scorecards. The ATS enforces this by hiding other interviewers' scores until yours is submitted. Overall scores are not averaged mechanically — they are discussed in the debrief (Section 4).

### Scored Dimensions

Dimensions vary by role, but every loop includes:
- **Relevant experience and skills** — does the candidate's background match the role's requirements?
- **Problem-solving and analytical thinking** — how does the candidate approach novel or ambiguous problems?
- **Communication and collaboration** — clarity of expression, listening, and ability to work across teams.
- **Values alignment** — evidence of behaviours consistent with GSVH Corp's stated values.
- **Growth trajectory** — for early-career roles: potential and rate of learning; for senior roles: evidence of increasing scope and impact.

Stage-specific dimensions (e.g., system design depth for senior engineers) are added to the scorecard template for those stages and documented by the recruiter before the loop opens.

## 4. Debrief Process

After Stage 4, the recruiter convenes a **debrief meeting** with all interviewers, typically within 2 business days. Each interviewer summarises their top finding (positive and concern) before scores are revealed. The hiring manager facilitates; the recruiter ensures everyone speaks before discussion begins.

Debrief rules:
- No reference to candidate attributes protected by law (age, gender, family status, disability, etc.).
- Concerns must be specific and tied to job-relevant evidence from the interview, not vague impressions.
- A 2:4 split (two No / two Yes) requires extended discussion, not a coin-flip; if consensus cannot be reached, escalate to the HR Business Partner.
- Strong No (score 1) from any interviewer automatically escalates to the hiring manager and HR for review — a single strong objection does not veto a hire but must be addressed explicitly.

The debrief outcome is recorded in the ATS as one of: Proceed to offer, Hold, or Decline.

## 5. Offer Approval Chain

Before an offer is extended, it must be approved through the following chain depending on the level of the role:

| Role Level | Approval Required |
|------------|-------------------|
| L1–L3 (Associate to Mid-level) | Hiring Manager |
| L4 (Senior) | Hiring Manager + Director |
| L5 (Staff / Lead) | Hiring Manager + Director + VP |
| **L6+ (Principal, Director+, VP+)** | **Hiring Manager + Director + VP + CFO** |

The CFO sign-off for L6 and above applies to all offers regardless of department. Offers for L6+ must include an approved compensation summary from Finance showing the offer sits within the approved band for the level. No verbal offer may be made until the written approval chain is complete.

The recruiter generates the offer letter from the approved template in the ATS and sends it for DocuSign. Offer letters expire after 5 business days unless extended by the hiring manager.

## 6. Background Checks and References

Background checks are commissioned after the verbal offer is accepted and before the written offer is countersigned. The scope follows `hr/employment-basics.md`: employment history and education for most roles, with additional financial or criminal checks for roles with financial authority or privileged system access.

Reference checks are conducted by the recruiter on at least two references provided by the candidate, at least one of whom must be a former direct manager. Reference check notes are recorded in the ATS.

A background check or reference concern does not automatically rescind an offer; HR conducts an individualized assessment, as defined in `hr/employment-basics.md`.

## 7. Interviewer Certification

Any employee who conducts a Stage 4 culture-and-values interview or who serves as a lead interviewer in a technical stage must complete GSVH Corp's interviewer training course (available in the LMS, approximately 2 hours). The training covers structured interviewing techniques, scoring calibration, bias awareness, and legal boundaries (questions that cannot be asked). Certification is recorded in Workday and must be renewed every 2 years.

Uncertified employees may shadow interviews but may not score candidates or participate in debriefs independently.

## 8. Data Handling

Candidate data in the ATS is confidential and subject to the data retention schedule in `legal-compliance/data-protection-and-privacy.md`. Declined candidates' data is retained for a maximum of 24 months for audit purposes and then purged. Interviewers must not keep personal copies of resumes, cover letters, or scorecard notes outside the ATS.
