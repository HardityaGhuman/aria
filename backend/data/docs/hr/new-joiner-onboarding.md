# New Joiner Onboarding — GSVH Corp

This document explains the onboarding journey for all new employees at GSVH Corp, covering pre-arrival setup, the 30/60/90-day milestone framework, the buddy program, probation expectations, and the resources available during your first three months. It applies to all new full-time and part-time employees globally. Contractors follow a lighter process coordinated by the hiring manager.

## 1. Pre-Arrival

Before your start date, HR sends a welcome email with instructions to complete employment forms, set up payroll, and enroll in benefits (where applicable). You will receive access to the GSVH Corp New Joiner Portal, which contains your offer letter, the employee handbook links, and a pre-reading list for your team.

IT processes your equipment request as soon as your start date is confirmed in the HRIS. The SLA for equipment readiness is **3 business days** from the date HR logs your start date; if your start date is fewer than 3 business days away, IT escalates to priority provisioning. Standard kit includes a company laptop, headset, and any peripherals specified by your manager. If you have accessibility requirements, flag them to HR and IT at least 5 business days before your start date so adjustments can be made.

A **buddy is assigned to every new joiner on or before Day 1.** Buddies are peer-level employees (not your manager) who have been at GSVH Corp for at least 6 months. They are your informal guide for the first 90 days — answering unwritten-rules questions, making introductions, and helping you navigate day-to-day life at the company. Your buddy's name and contact details will be in your welcome email.

## 2. Day 1

On Day 1 your manager meets with you for at least 1 hour to walk through your role, introduce the team, and set context for your first week. You will also attend a company orientation session — run by HR — that covers GSVH Corp's mission, organizational structure, core policies (code of conduct, data protection, acceptable use), and how to find help.

IT onboarding runs in parallel: your accounts (Okta SSO, company email, Slack) are live by the time you arrive. If anything is missing, contact the IT service desk via the onboarding ticket created for your name. Badge access for your primary office location is activated by the end of Day 1.

## 3. The 30/60/90-Day Framework

GSVH Corp structures the first 90 days around three milestones. The manager and new joiner agree on specific goals for each milestone during the Day 1 meeting; these are logged in Workday under the onboarding plan.

### 30-Day Milestone: Learn

By Day 30, you should understand your team's function and how it connects to the broader company, have met your immediate colleagues and key cross-functional partners, and begun contributing to at least one live project or workstream. The focus is learning — reading documentation, shadowing teammates, attending team rituals, and asking questions.

Your manager conducts a structured 30-day check-in. Topics: how you are settling in, any blockers or unmet access needs, initial impressions of the role versus your expectations, and whether the buddy relationship is working. This check-in is logged but not part of the formal performance record.

### 60-Day Milestone: Contribute

By Day 60, you should be independently contributing to your team's work, owning at least one well-scoped task end-to-end, and visible in cross-functional meetings relevant to your role. You should have submitted your first expense claim (if applicable), completed any mandatory training (security awareness, code of conduct, data protection), and be comfortable with GSVH Corp's main tools.

Your manager conducts a structured 60-day check-in to review your onboarding plan progress, recalibrate your 90-day goals if needed, and surface any emerging concerns early. If there are performance concerns at 60 days, HR must be looped in before the 90-day review.

### 90-Day Milestone: Own

By Day 90 — the end of the probation period — you should be operating with a reasonable degree of independence, have a clear personal roadmap for the next quarter, and understand how your work is measured. The 90-day review is the formal end-of-probation conversation.

The manager completes the probation review form in Workday and shares a written outcome with the employee: successful completion, extension (up to 30 additional days, documented), or — in rare cases — separation. Successful completion is confirmed in writing by HR and does not change the employment contract type.

## 4. Probation Period

All new hires serve a **90 calendar-day probation period** from their start date. During probation, the notice period on both sides is one week rather than the standard notice period described in `hr/employment-basics.md`. Both parties have this shorter notice to reflect that the role and the fit are still being evaluated.

Probation may be extended once by up to 30 days where additional time is genuinely needed; the extension must be documented in writing and shared with the employee before Day 90. If no formal extension is issued by Day 89, probation ends automatically on Day 90.

## 5. Mandatory Training

New joiners must complete the following mandatory training within their first 30 days:
- Security awareness (IT, 45 minutes, online)
- Code of conduct and ethics (HR, 30 minutes, online)
- Data protection and privacy (IT/Legal, 30 minutes, online)
- Anti-harassment acknowledgement (HR, 15 minutes, sign and return)

Completion is tracked in the LMS. Incomplete mandatory training by Day 30 is escalated to the manager. Some locations require additional local-law modules; HR confirms which apply to your entity.

## 6. Buddy Program Details

The buddy relationship runs for the first 90 days. Buddies are expected to:
- Meet with the new joiner at least once a week for the first month, then bi-weekly through Day 90.
- Make introductions to people outside the new joiner's immediate team.
- Share informal knowledge about how GSVH Corp works — unwritten norms, team culture, the best way to get things done.
- Flag to HR (confidentially) if the new joiner appears to be struggling and has not yet raised concerns with their manager.

Buddies receive 1 hour of protected time per week in their first-month commitment. This is tracked in Workday and credited toward their own contribution goals. Managers nominate buddies for new joiners at least 5 business days before the start date; HR maintains a buddy roster and rotates assignments to spread the load.

## 7. Tools and Access

Your full access stack is provisioned by IT during onboarding:
- **Okta** — single sign-on for all GSVH Corp applications.
- **Workday** — HR self-service (pay stubs, PTO requests, onboarding plan).
- **Slack** — primary messaging. You will be added to your team's channels and the #new-joiners channel by your buddy.
- **GitHub** (engineering roles) or **Google Workspace** (all roles) — as applicable.
- **1Password** — company password manager; mandatory for all employees.

Additional role-specific tools are provisioned by the manager or IT within the first week. If access to a tool is missing after Day 3, raise a service desk ticket — do not ask colleagues to share credentials, even temporarily.

## 8. Support During Onboarding

If you are struggling — with the role, the tools, the culture, or anything else — there are multiple channels:
- **Your buddy:** best for informal, day-to-day questions.
- **Your manager:** best for role clarity, workload, and performance expectations.
- **HR:** for anything sensitive, contractual, or that involves your manager.
- **EAP (Employee Assistance Program):** confidential counselling and support; details in `benefits/health-and-wellbeing.md`.

GSVH Corp treats a difficult first 90 days as a signal to be investigated and addressed, not as evidence that the hire was a mistake. Speak up early; problems caught at Day 30 are far easier to resolve than problems surfaced at Day 89.
