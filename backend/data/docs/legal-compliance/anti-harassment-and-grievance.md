# Anti-Harassment and Grievance Policy — GSVH Corp

This policy defines GSVH Corp's commitment to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and bullying, and sets out the process for raising and resolving a grievance. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties working at or visiting GSVH Corp premises or participating in GSVH Corp activities. The Code of Conduct in `hr/code-of-conduct.md` sets related behavioural expectations.

## 1. Our Commitment

GSVH Corp does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or bullying in any form. Every employee has the right to work in an environment that is respectful, inclusive, and free from intimidation. Violations of this policy are taken seriously regardless of seniority, and there is no context — after-work events, team messaging channels, external conferences — where this policy does not apply.

Leadership is accountable for culture. Managers who fail to address or report harassment, or who create a hostile environment for their team, face the same disciplinary consequences as the individuals who commit the behaviour.

## 2. Definitions

**Harassment** is unwanted conduct related to a protected characteristic — including gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, race, nationality, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law — that has the purpose or effect of violating someone's dignity or creating an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment.

**Sexual harassment** is a specific and serious form of harassment: unwanted conduct of a sexual nature. This includes comments, jokes, touching, requests for sexual favours, sharing sexually explicit material, or any other conduct of a sexual nature that the recipient finds unwanted. In India, the Prevention of Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH) Act 2013 applies to all GSVH India Pvt Ltd employees; the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) process under POSH runs in parallel with and supplements this policy for India-based complaints.

**Bullying** is repeated, unreasonable behaviour directed at an individual or group that creates a risk of physical or psychological harm. Constructive feedback, holding people to performance standards, and restructuring decisions are not bullying.

**Discrimination** is treating someone less favourably because of a protected characteristic, or applying a requirement, condition, or practice that disproportionately disadvantages a protected group without objective justification.

## 3. Reporting Channels

GSVH Corp provides multiple channels for reporting harassment or raising a grievance. You should use whichever channel you are most comfortable with.

| Channel | Contact | Use For |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Your manager | Direct | Informal resolution of workplace disputes and minor grievances (not for harassment by the manager). |
| HR Business Partner | `hr@gsvhcorp.com` | Formal complaints, harassment, or issues involving your manager. |
| Ethics Hotline | Anonymous, available 24/7 at `ethics.gsvhcorp.com` | Anonymous reports of any policy violation, including harassment, fraud, or misconduct. |
| Legal / General Counsel | `legal@gsvhcorp.com` | Reports involving an executive or senior leader; potential legal exposure. |
| India ICC (POSH) | Bengaluru HR, ICC contact list on intranet | Sexual harassment complaints for India employees (mandatory route under POSH). |

You may report on your own behalf or on behalf of a colleague (third-party report). You are never required to confront the person about whom you are reporting before making a formal complaint.

**No retaliation:** GSVH Corp strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who makes a complaint in good faith, cooperates with an investigation, or opposes a discriminatory or harassing act. Retaliation is itself a serious conduct violation subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 4. Acknowledgement of Receipt

Every formal grievance or harassment complaint received by HR — through any of the channels above — will receive a written **acknowledgement within 2 business days** of receipt. The acknowledgement confirms the complaint has been received, names the person responsible for the investigation, and outlines the process and expected timeline.

Anonymous reports via the Ethics Hotline receive a response posted to the anonymous reporter's follow-up code within 2 business days. Provide your follow-up code when you make the report so you can check for the response.

## 5. Investigation Process

Formal complaints are investigated impartially, promptly, and confidentially. The investigation is conducted by HR (or, for senior-level complaints, an external investigator retained by Legal). The investigator:

1. Interviews the complainant to understand the full facts.
2. Interviews the subject of the complaint and gives them a fair opportunity to respond.
3. Interviews relevant witnesses.
4. Reviews documentary evidence (messages, emails, calendar records).

**The investigation will be completed within 30 calendar days of the formal complaint being logged.** If a particularly complex case requires additional time, the complainant is notified in writing before Day 30 with an updated expected timeline and the reasons for the extension.

Both the complainant and the subject of the complaint are kept informed of the investigation's status at regular intervals. Neither party is expected to work in proximity to the other during the investigation if that would cause distress; HR coordinates appropriate interim arrangements (shift changes, remote work for the period, or temporary reassignment where necessary).

## 6. Interim Measures

Where the seriousness of a complaint warrants it, GSVH Corp may put interim measures in place during the investigation to protect the complainant or the integrity of the investigation. These may include:
- Requiring the subject of the complaint to work remotely or in a different location.
- Temporarily suspending the subject of the complaint on full pay, pending the investigation outcome.
- Placing communication restrictions between the parties.

Interim measures are not a finding of guilt. They are protective steps taken to allow a fair investigation. Interim measures are reviewed weekly by HR and adjusted as the investigation progresses.

## 7. Outcomes and Disciplinary Action

At the conclusion of the investigation, the investigator prepares a written findings report. Findings are one of:
- **Substantiated:** the complaint is supported by the evidence. Disciplinary action follows under `hr/termination-and-discipline.md`, ranging from a formal written warning to termination depending on severity.
- **Partially substantiated:** some elements of the complaint are supported. HR determines the appropriate response.
- **Unsubstantiated:** the evidence does not support the complaint. The complaint is closed; the subject of the complaint is informed and all interim measures are lifted.

An unsubstantiated finding does not mean the complainant was dishonest; it means the evidence was insufficient to reach a substantiated conclusion. The complainant may appeal the outcome within 10 business days of receiving the findings, as described in Section 8. A complaint made maliciously or in demonstrably bad faith is itself a conduct violation, distinct from an unsubstantiated complaint.

Both parties receive a summary of the outcome in writing. The full investigation report is confidential and is held by HR; it is disclosed only to the disciplinary decision-maker and, where legally required, to the parties themselves.

## 8. Appeal

Either the complainant or the subject of the complaint may appeal the investigation outcome. Appeals must be submitted in writing to the HR Director within **10 business days** of receiving the outcome summary. Grounds for appeal are limited to:
- Procedural irregularity (the investigation was not conducted fairly).
- New material evidence not available during the investigation.
- The outcome is disproportionate to the findings.

Appeals are considered by a senior HR leader not involved in the original investigation. The appeal decision is issued within 15 business days and is final.

## 9. Informal Resolution

Not every workplace conflict requires a formal investigation. Minor interpersonal tensions, one-off miscommunications, or low-level friction are often better resolved through facilitated conversation between the parties, with HR or a manager as a neutral facilitator. HR offers informal mediation for complaints where both parties consent and the conduct does not include harassment or serious misconduct.

Choosing informal resolution does not prevent a party from escalating to a formal complaint if the informal process does not resolve the issue.

## 10. Record-Keeping

Formal complaints, investigation records, and outcomes are retained in the employee's HR file for 7 years from the date the complaint is closed, consistent with the retention schedule in `legal-compliance/data-protection-and-privacy.md`. Anonymous Ethics Hotline reports are retained in the Ethics Hotline system for the same period. Records are treated as Restricted data and are accessible only to HR, Legal, and the investigation team.
